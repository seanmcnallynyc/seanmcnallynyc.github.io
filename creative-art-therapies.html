Creative Art Therapies

Creative Arts Therapies is an umbrella term for healthcare professions that use the creative and expressive process of art making to support an individual’s overall wellbeing, psychosocial, physical and spiritual needs. Creative art therapies include the following modalities delivered by a credential professional.

Music Therapy

The use of music to promote wellbeing and support an individual’s overall wellbeing at any stage of life with clinical applications of music and a therapeutic relationship.

Source: www.musictherapy.org

Art Therapy

A mental health profession that enriches lives of individuals, families, and communities through active art-making, creative process, applied psychological theory, and human experience within a therapeutic relationship.

Source: www.nyarttherapy.org

Dance / Movement Therapy

The psychotherapeutic use of movement to promote emotional, social, cognitive, and physical integration of the individual.

Source: www.adta.org

Drama Therapy

An embodied practice that is active and experiential and can provide the context for participants to tell their stories, set goals and solve problems, express feelings, or achieve catharsis.

Source: www.nadta.org

Poetry / Bibliotherapy

Uses language, symbol, and story in therapeutic, educational growth, and community-building capacities and relies upon the use of poems, stories, song lyrics, imagery, and metaphor to facilitate personal growth, healing, and greater self-awareness.

Source: www.poetrytherapy.org

Intermodal Expressive Arts

Embraces the connection and interplay of the various arts modalities, including all forms of visual art, photography, craft, movement/dance/somatics, voice/rhythm/sound/music, drama, writing, guided meditation, imagination, and nature-based practices.

Uses two or more arts modalities, typically sequenced or layered. An experienced expressive arts facilitator or therapist guides the effective interweaving of modalities to provide maximum benefit to the client.

Source: www.ieata.org
